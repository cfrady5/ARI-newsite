Our Process

How ARI builds and operates initiatives

Every initiative follows the same disciplined arc—from understanding the mission to scaling the impact—so good ideas don't stall on the way to delivery.

Identify mission needs

We start with the agency, regional, and industry priorities that define what success must look like.

Align the right partners

As a neutral convener, we bring government, industry, and academia to the same table around shared outcomes.

Structure the program

We design the governance, funding, and mechanisms required to turn intent into an executable program.

Launch the pathway

We stand up and operate the marketplace, consortium, or event that moves work from idea to award.

Measure and scale impact

We track outcomes—jobs, awards, capabilities transitioned—and grow what works into national-scale impact.

Have an initiative idea or a capability to bring forward?

Whether you represent a government program, a company with a breakthrough, or a university with research to transition—ARI can help you find the fastest path to impact.
