What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide information to the site’s operators. Cookies may be “session” cookies, which are deleted when you close your browser, or “persistent” cookies, which remain on your device until they expire or you delete them. We also use similar technologies—such as pixels, tags, and local storage—which we refer to collectively as “cookies” throughout this Policy.

Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies on the Site:

  • Strictly necessary cookies. These cookies are essential for the Site to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They enable core functionality such as page navigation, security, and access to secure areas. The Site cannot work properly without them.
  • Functionality & preference cookies. These cookies allow the Site to remember choices you make—such as your language or display preferences—to provide a more personalized and consistent experience. They are not essential, but they improve usability.
  • Analytics & performance cookies. These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the Site by collecting and reporting information anonymously or in aggregate—for example, which pages are most visited and whether visitors encounter errors. We use this information to measure and improve the performance of the Site.

We do not use advertising or targeting cookies on the Site. If we introduce advertising cookies in the future, we will update this Policy and, where required, request your consent before they are set.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to operate and secure the Site, to remember your preferences, and to understand how the Site is used so we can improve our content and user experience. Analytics cookies help us see which pages and resources are most useful so we can prioritize our work. We do not use cookies to sell your personal information.

Managing Your Preferences

You have several ways to control cookies:

  • Browser settings. Most web browsers let you view, manage, block, and delete cookies through their settings. Refer to your browser’s help resources to learn how. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may cause parts of the Site to stop working correctly.
  • Cookie banner. Where a cookie banner or preference manager is presented on the Site, you can use it to accept or decline non-essential cookies and to change your choices at any time.

Because your preferences are stored in a cookie, you may need to set them again if you clear your cookies or use a different browser or device.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party service providers we use—for example, analytics providers that help us measure Site performance. These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy and cookie practices. We encourage you to review the policies of any third-party providers to understand how they use cookies and the data they collect.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Please revisit this page periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies.
