Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026.

This document is provided as a template and should be reviewed by legal counsel before publication.

The Applied Research Institute (“ARI,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you visit theari.us (the “Site”), subscribe to our communications, or otherwise interact with us.

Overview & Scope

This Policy applies to personal information we collect through the Site and through related activities such as our newsletter, event registrations, and inquiry forms. It does not apply to third-party websites, services, or platforms that we do not control, even where we link to them.

By using the Site, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please discontinue your use of the Site. We may provide additional or supplemental privacy notices for specific programs or initiatives; where a supplemental notice conflicts with this Policy, the supplemental notice controls for that activity.

Information We Collect

We collect two broad categories of information: information that is collected automatically when you use the Site, and information that you choose to provide to us.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, certain information is collected automatically through your browser, device, and our analytics tools. This may include:

  • Your Internet Protocol (IP) address and approximate, derived location;
  • Device and browser characteristics, such as device type, operating system, browser type and version, language settings, and screen resolution;
  • Usage data, such as the pages you view, the links you click, referring and exit pages, the dates and times of your visits, and how you navigate the Site;
  • Diagnostic and performance data that helps us keep the Site reliable and secure.

Much of this information is gathered through cookies and similar technologies. See Cookies & Tracking below.

Information you provide to us

When you complete a form, subscribe to our newsletter, register for an event, or otherwise contact us, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name;
  • Your email address;
  • Your organization or affiliation;
  • Your ZIP or postal code;
  • Any message, inquiry, or other details you choose to share with us.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for legitimate purposes connected to ARI’s mission and operations, including to:

  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site;
  • Respond to your inquiries, requests, and partnership or program interest;
  • Send you the ARI Research & Innovation Report and other communications you have requested, including program updates, funding opportunities, and event invitations;
  • Understand how the Site is used so we can improve our content, services, and user experience;
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or misuse; and
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and lawful requests, and to protect our rights and the rights of others.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site, remember your preferences, and understand how the Site is used. You can learn more about the specific cookies we use, why we use them, and how to manage your preferences in our Cookie Policy.

How We Share Information

ARI does not sell your personal information. We share information only in the limited circumstances described below:

  • Service providers and processors. We share information with trusted vendors who perform services on our behalf—such as website hosting, email delivery, analytics, and event management—under contractual obligations of confidentiality and data protection. These providers may use your information only to perform services for us.
  • Legal and safety requirements. We may disclose information where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a lawful governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of ARI, our partners, or the public.
  • Organizational changes. If ARI is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, consistent with this Policy.

We do not, and will not, sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, including to provide the communications and services you have requested, to comply with our legal and contractual obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete it or to de-identify it. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the context in which it was collected.

Data Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping any credentials you use to interact with us confidential.

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Delete certain personal information; and
  • Unsubscribe from our marketing communications.

You can unsubscribe at any time by using the “unsubscribe” link in any email we send, or by contacting us directly. To exercise any other right, email us at privacy@theari.us with your request. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and consistent with applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites, resources, and services that we do not operate or control. This Policy does not apply to those third parties, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

Children’s Privacy

The Site is intended for a general, professional audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@theari.us so we can take appropriate steps to delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Your continued use of the Site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, or to direct any privacy or legal inquiry, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@theari.us
  • Mailing address: Applied Research Institute, 16 Tech Innovation District, Indianapolis, Indiana
