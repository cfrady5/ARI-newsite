Who We Are

ARI gets complex innovation work moving.

We are the neutral operating partner for mission-driven innovation—rooted in Indiana and trusted across the nation. ARI eliminates friction, builds the pathways that move work forward, and operates the mechanisms that connect government to real solutions.

Explore our initiatives Work with us
Our Role

The neutral partner that turns priorities into execution.

ARI sits between government priorities, private-sector capability, academic expertise, and regional assets—aligning partners around shared outcomes and operating the mechanisms required to execute.

We don't compete with our partners. As a neutral, third-party nonprofit, ARI is trusted to convene rivals, structure programs that serve the mission first, and move work forward when traditional pathways stall. The result is disciplined execution and national-scale impact, delivered with the urgency the mission demands.

Rooted in Indiana and trusted across the nation: ARI gets things done.

How We Work

Four operating principles

Every program ARI builds and runs is grounded in the same commitments.

Neutrality

We are a trusted, third-party convener—able to bring competitors and agencies to the same table without a stake in the outcome.

Execution

We operate the mechanisms—programs, marketplaces, and pathways—that turn intent into delivered capability.

Speed

We compress timelines because the mission can't wait—reducing the lag between a good idea and a fielded solution.

Impact

We measure success in outcomes—jobs created, capabilities transitioned, dollars deployed, and missions advanced.

Our History

A legacy of impact.

For nearly a decade, ARI has evolved to meet its partners' needs—growing from a bridge between Indiana's universities and federal labs into a national operator of innovation programs and tech hubs.

  • 2017

    ARI is founded

    Established to bridge Indiana's research universities and Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane.

  • 2021

    Rapid Acquisition Marketplace launches

    ARI stands up RAM and executes its first major Department of Defense contracts.

  • 2023

    Federal Tech Hub designations

    Indiana is selected for multiple federal Tech Hub designations spanning microelectronics and biosecurity.

  • 2024

    $150M+ secured for tech hubs

    ARI's programs secure more than $150 million in federal funding across three regional tech hubs.

  • 2025

    Andrew Kossack named CEO

    ARI promotes Andrew Kossack to Chief Executive Officer & President to lead its next phase of national growth.
