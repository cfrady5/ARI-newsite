ARI Promotes Andrew Kossack to Chief Executive Officer

September 19, 2025 · 4 min read · By ARI Communications

The Applied Research Institute (ARI) today announced the promotion of Andrew Kossack to Chief Executive Officer. Kossack, who previously served as Executive Vice President of Partnerships and General Counsel, steps into the role at the close of one of the most consequential growth periods in the institute's history.

A leader shaped by public service

Kossack brings to the role a career spanning federal and state government and the private sector. He has held positions with U.S. Senator Todd Young, led at the Indiana Department of Revenue, and served in the White House—experience that has given him a working command of how policy, procurement, and partnership intersect.

That background maps directly to ARI's mission. The institute exists to sit between government priorities, private-sector capability, and academic depth, and to operate the mechanisms that turn intent into delivered outcomes. Leading that work demands someone fluent in all three worlds.

The mission is bigger than any single program—it's building the connective tissue that lets the country move faster.

A period of rapid growth

Since 2023, ARI has expanded its national footprint at a remarkable pace. The institute helped secure federal tech-hub designations spanning microelectronics and biosecurity, establishing Indiana as a recognized center of gravity for two of the most strategically important technology domains in the country.

In parallel, ARI entered into a five-year, $600 million cooperative agreement with the Defense Innovation Unit—an agreement that cements the institute's role as a trusted operator at the intersection of defense and commercial innovation.

  • Federal tech-hub designations in microelectronics and biosecurity.
  • A five-year, $600 million cooperative agreement with the Defense Innovation Unit.
  • Continued growth of the programs and pathways that move innovation to national scale.

What comes next

As Chief Executive Officer, Kossack will lead ARI's next phase of national growth—scaling proven programs, deepening partnerships across government and industry, and holding the institute to its core standard: outcomes, not overhead. The promotion reflects both the trajectory ARI has been on and the discipline required to sustain it.
