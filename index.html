Our Mission

Applied solutions for transformational ideas.

We catalyze opportunities that enhance national security and drive economic prosperity.

As a neutral, third-party nonprofit, ARI sits between government priorities, private-sector capability, academic depth, and regional assets—aligning partners around shared outcomes and operating the mechanisms required to execute.
